# CoachKit hard rules

These rules are non-negotiable. Every programming update must keep them.

## Goalkeeper plays a full period

The goalkeeper plays the entire period. A period is a quarter (4), a third (3), or a half (2). No substitution takes the goalkeeper off during that period.

- Do not plan, queue, or run a sub that takes the goalkeeper off during a period.
- Do not bring a bench player on for the goalkeeper during a period.
- Do not drag the goalkeeper off, and do not drag someone into the goalkeeper spot, while sub mode is on or once the period clock has started.
- Fair-play, scramble, and redraw leave the goalkeeper in goal for the whole period. The goalkeeper does not play half of a period in goal.
- A different goalkeeper is allowed only between periods, for the whole next period. Set that in the lineup before the period starts.
- When the age or custom setup has no goalkeeper, there is no `GK` slot and this rule does not invent one.

The goalkeeper is the player in the `GK` slot.
