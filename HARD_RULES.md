# CoachKit hard rules

These rules are non-negotiable. Every programming update must keep them.

## Goalkeeper plays a full quarter

The goalkeeper plays the entire quarter. No mid-quarter substitution for the goalkeeper.

- Do not plan, queue, or run a sub that takes the goalkeeper off during a quarter.
- Do not bring a bench player on for the goalkeeper during a quarter.
- Do not drag the goalkeeper off, and do not drag someone into the goalkeeper spot, while sub mode is on or once the quarter clock has started.
- Fair-play, scramble, and redraw leave the goalkeeper in goal for the whole quarter. The goalkeeper does not play a half-quarter in goal.
- A different goalkeeper is allowed only between quarters, for the whole next quarter. Set that in the full-quarter lineup before the quarter starts.

The goalkeeper is the player in the `GK` slot.
